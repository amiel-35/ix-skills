# Reference: Value vs Effort

**Use:** quick backlog sort, and the default in degraded mode (no goal, no data): it needs only what the item descriptions show.

## Axes
- **Value** — benefit toward the goal (or, in degraded mode, the benefit the item itself states). High / Low, or 1–5.
- **Effort** — work and dependencies implied by the description. High / Low, or 1–5.

## Quadrants and order
1. **Quick wins** — high value, low effort: do first.
2. **Big bets** — high value, high effort: plan, sequence, maybe split.
3. **Fill-ins** — low value, low effort: only when capacity is idle.
4. **Money pits** — low value, high effort: park or drop, with a reason.

Inside a quadrant, order by value ÷ effort when scored 1–5.

## Degraded-mode reading of descriptions
- Effort signals: "migrate", "rewrite", "integrate with", "all", several teams named, unknown technology → higher.
- Value signals: customer-facing, revenue or risk words, blocking other items → higher.
Mark every such score `[assumed]`.

## Pitfalls
- Most items land in "big bets" — split them or use a finer framework.
- Effort estimated optimistically for exciting items.
- The 2×2 hides how close an item is to the line: show the 1–5 scores when you have them.
