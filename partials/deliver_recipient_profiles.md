# Reference: Recipient profiles for delivery

## Target profiles

- DSI (IT director): risks, costs, governance, little fine-grained technical detail
- dev: technical specs, constraints, examples, little business varnish
- sponsor: ROI, schedule, business risk, very concise format
- client: concrete benefits, impacts, little technical jargon
- tech lead: architecture trade-offs, potential debt
- linkedin: strong hook, airy structure, little jargon
- email: clear subject line, short message, a single action requested

## Usage rule

If a profile is provided, apply it explicitly.
Otherwise, choose the most plausible profile based on the brief and the type of deliverable, then flag it in the frontmatter or the summary.
